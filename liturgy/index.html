The rhythm of the Mass

A guide for attention as the reading and prayer change throughout the year.

01 · GATHER

Introductory rites

Gather as one, ask for mercy, and prepare to hear the Word.

02 · LISTEN

Liturgy of the Word

Readings, responsorial psalm, Gospel acclamation, Gospel, homily, and prayer.

03 · GIVE THANKS

Liturgy of the Eucharist

Offer, give thanks, pray, and receive. Follow your parish's celebration.

04 · GO FORTH

Concluding rites

Carry what you heard into a concrete act of mercy.

Psalm & acclamation

The responsorial psalm belongs to the Liturgy of the Word. Its response may be sung or recited. The Gospel acclamation also comes from the day’s readings; your parish chooses its other music.
