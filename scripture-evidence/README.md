# Scripture Evidence & Discovery — Catholic Companion

Static-first research workspace at /scripture-evidence/.

## Current capabilities
- Claim ledger with evidence status, source references, reasoning, limitations, and counterevidence.
- Source registry with provenance, edition, language, date, URL/locator, and rights notes.
- Place/event records, schematic coordinate display, and filterable timeline.
- Research questions and next-evidence steps.
- Method rules and doctrinal-status distinctions.
- Browser-local persistence and JSON backup/restore.

## Limits
Records are local to the current browser and do not sync. No automatic source fetching, full-text indexing, OCR, manuscript collation, or scholarly verification is implemented. The schematic map is not a historical basemap. Starter records are research prompts, not verified scholarship. The existing timeline and Solarium data have not yet been bulk-ingested.

## Integrity
Preserve original artifacts. Treat imports as versioned source packages. Validate IDs and relationships before mapping. Keep evidence, inference, interpretation, and doctrinal authority separate.
