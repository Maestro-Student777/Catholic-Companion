6 AM · North

The pointer starts at blue north, 6:00 AM and completes one clockwise turn in 24 hours. The zones below are a creative color system, not astronomical spectral measurements.

Geography and chronology remain separate

How the dots work

Dot positions are latitude and longitude. You can turn the globe freely without moving the underlying locations. Select a dot to read its story, or select a story to bring its location into view.

From earlier projects

The 353 numbered names were read from our CC-TL-002 Adam → Leo XIV source. Its original order is reproduced as a dedicated story collection. The Bible & Christianity view is a smaller event chronology with named places, Council sites, and selected MT reference years.

Why some events have no dot

Adam, Seth, Enoch and many genealogical names have no verified coordinates. Mapping them to a modern city would confuse theological narrative with geographic evidence.

Events save in this browser when allowed and are also included in the main Cabinet backup. New story data is kept separate from the permanent P-000 palette.
